About Me

Hello, I'm Nolawit

I'm a fourth-year Architecture student at Unity University in Addis Ababa, passionate about creating spaces that are functional, sustainable, and people-centered. My design philosophy is rooted in biophilic principles: architecture that lives in harmony with nature and enhances human well-being.

I'm fascinated by the intersection of neuroscience and design, exploring how the built environment shapes our emotions, behavior, and health, especially within Ethiopia's unique culture, climate, and resources. Guided by a love for art, I aim to create architecture that is innovative, meaningful, and deeply connected to the natural world.

Beyond design, I serve as Executive Secretary of NAASE, a role entrusted to me through the students' vote. I've maintained a 4.0 GPA, served as class representative, and founded both an art club and an academic volunteer team.
